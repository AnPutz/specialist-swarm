# Request for Proposal: New Platform "Digital Wish List 2027"

**Client:** Santa Claus Logistics & Gift-Giving Association, 1 North Pole, 99999 North Pole
**Contact:** Mr S. Claus, Chief Gifting Officer
**Deadline:** 30 November, 23:59 North Pole Time

## 1. Background
We have run worldwide gift-giving since 1823. Wish lists arrive by letter, chimney and occasionally
by fax. In 2026 we received 2.1 billion wish lists; 14 % arrived late or charred.
Our current shop solution (in-house development, COBOL, operated by two reindeer) is being replaced.

## 2. Goals
- Digital wish-list platform for 2 billion children in 190 countries and 140 languages.
- Wish-list checkout with a check against the naughty-or-nice list.
- Live sleigh tracking for parents ("Where is my present?").
- Go-live no later than 1 October 2027.

## 3. Volumes
- Peak load: 24 December between 18:00 and 20:00 local time, rolling across all time zones.
- Expected: 40 million concurrent users, 900,000 orders per minute.
- Catalogue: 12 million toy items, 30 % of them made in our own workshop.

## 4. Interfaces
- Workshop ERP "ElfERP" (SOAP, reachable on Tuesdays only).
- Reindeer logistics via OCC API, including route optimisation per chimney.
- Payment: none; children pay in cookies and milk (must be recorded).

## 5. Data Protection and Security
- All data stays at the North Pole (no transfer to third countries, not even to the South Pole).
- The naughty-or-nice list is maintained all year; "he sees you when you're sleeping".
- Read-only access for Knecht Ruprecht.

## 6. Contract Terms
- Unlimited liability of the contractor for any outage on 24 December.
- Penalty per minute of outage: 1 tonne of coal.
- Payment preferably in cookies; euros possible by agreement.
- Fixed price.
